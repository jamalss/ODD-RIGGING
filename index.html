Ordering

How to order

  1. Pick a design and size

    Choose your size on any design, then add it to your WhatsApp order. Add as many designs as you like.

  2. Send your order on WhatsApp

    Your order is written into a WhatsApp message for you. Add your name and delivery area, then send it. We confirm your order on WhatsApp.

  3. Delivery or pickup, pay in cash

    Islandwide delivery in Saint Lucia within 2–3 days for US$1.84, or free pickup. Pay cash on delivery or at pickup. All prices are in USD.

Outside Saint Lucia? Order through TeePublic using the Buy on TeePublic button on each design or contact us via WhatsApp (opens WhatsApp) or Email for more info.
